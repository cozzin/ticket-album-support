Using Ticket Album

  • Choose Add photos to album and select photos. They are arranged, saved automatically and opened in your album. No title or editing step is required. Camera and files are available in More.
  • Open a collected piece to straighten paper or remove its background. Your original is kept.
  • Open Edit when you want to decorate. Use the materials button to open the optional Collection or Photos tray. Drag a piece onto the page, or tap to add it. Chosen photos are saved in Collection automatically. Tap a piece to adjust it. The floating trash button removes it from the page only; Undo restores it. The nearby tools let you duplicate a piece or open precise adjustments.
  • The materials tray starts hidden so the page stays clear. Use the materials button below the page to open or hide it. In the page editor, select a photo and tap the nearby scissors to remove its background. Applying creates a separate piece, keeping other placements unchanged. Tap the text button to type directly on the album, with font, color and size controls above the keyboard. Select text and tap the pencil to edit in place again.
  • Dates are movable stickers. Settings → Automatically add a date sticker controls new pages. You can always add one in Edit → More → Add date sticker, then select it and use the pencil to change its date and style. Existing dates are preserved; removed date stickers do not return.
  • Share directly without a review checkbox.

Storage and recovery

The album is stored on your device. There is no account or cross-device synchronization. Device backups may include app data according to your system settings. Back up your device and export important pages before deleting the app. Removing a piece from a page does not delete its collected original.

Background removal

Results depend on lighting, contrast and the subject. Use the manual outline, erase and restore tools to refine the result, including hands or unwanted background. The original stays unchanged. Processing happens on your device.

Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Operator: Boksu Hong

Ticket Album does not collect personal data. It has no user account, analytics, advertising, tracking SDK, or app-operated server. Photos, titles, dates, edits and scrapbook pages are processed and stored locally on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to photograph a keepsake.
  • Recent Photos: opening the editor’s Photos tab requests photo-library access to show recent thumbnails. You can allow selected photos or full access. Only photos you tap, select or drop are copied into the local Collection and album; the library is not bulk-imported or uploaded.
  • Photo selection: the system photo picker also lets you choose specific images without granting full library access.
  • Add to Photos: requested only when you choose to save an exported page.

Sharing and backups

Sharing passes only your chosen exported PNG or PDF to an app or destination you select through the system share sheet. That destination’s privacy policy applies. Ticket codes and personal information can appear in your images; review and cover them before sharing. System device backups are controlled by your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy terms.

Purchases

The core app is free: unlimited local collection, background removal, paper correction, scrapbook editing and standard PNG sharing. Optional Ticket Album Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) adding high-resolution PNG, print-ready PDF and standalone piece PNG sharing. The App Store shows your local price before payment. Apple processes payment; the app verifies signed purchase history on-device to unlock Pro. We do not receive your payment card details or send transaction history to our own server. Restore purchases in Settings → Pro with the same Apple Account. Restoring Pro does not transfer your album. Refunds are managed by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple standard EULA applies.

Deleting your data

You can remove pages and collected pieces inside the app. Pieces referenced by a page must first be removed from those pages. Deleting the app removes its local data; manage any device backups separately in system settings. Exported images are managed in Photos, Files, or the service you shared them to.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email appsbyernest@gmail.com. If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
